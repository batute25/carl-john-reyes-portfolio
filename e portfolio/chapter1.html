Chapter I: Introduction

A. Importance of Internship

Molding Future IT Professionals

Internship is a crucial part of every Information Technology student’s journey. It serves as the bridge between academic learning and the real working environment.

While school teaches theories, concepts, and technical foundations, internship provides the opportunity to apply those lessons in actual workplace situations. It exposes students to real challenges that cannot be fully learned through books alone.

This experience helps improve not only technical skills but also personal character. Students learn teamwork, time management, communication, responsibility, and how to work under pressure.

Internship also helps students discover which field of IT they are most interested in, such as programming, networking, technical support, or system development.

Overall, internship contributes to both professional and personal growth. It gives students real industry exposure and prepares them to face future careers with more confidence, discipline, and direction after graduation.

B. Objectives of Internship

Develop Industry-Ready Professionals

The internship aims to provide practical experience in the field of Information Technology through real tasks and responsibilities.

It helps trainees understand actual workflows, industry practices, and workplace expectations.

Technical Experience: To gain hands-on experience in troubleshooting, hardware and software assistance, documentation, encoding, and laboratory-related technical tasks.

Career Foundation: To build a strong foundation for future careers such as technical support staff, web developer, programmer, system analyst, or other IT-related professions.

Problem-Solving Skills: To improve analytical thinking and problem-solving skills by identifying technical issues and providing effective solutions.

Documentation Skills: To develop the ability to prepare reports, records, inventories, and other important documents.

Collaboration Experience: To experience teamwork with supervisors, staff members, and fellow trainees while practicing professional communication.

Professional Growth: To enhance discipline, confidence, responsibility, adaptability, and work ethics needed in a professional environment.

Overall, the internship prepares the trainee for future careers in Information Technology by improving both technical and interpersonal skills through real-world experience.

C. Time and Place of Internship

My On-the-Job Training started in January 2026 and ended in April 2026.

The internship was conducted at the Occidental Mindoro State College (OMSC) Laboratory, San Jose Campus.

During this period, I was assigned to assist in laboratory operations, technical support tasks, equipment handling, documentation, and other responsibilities related to the daily functions of the laboratory.

Role: Student Trainee
Working Days: Monday to Friday
Work Setting: OMSC Laboratory (On-site Training)

This experience allowed me to apply the knowledge I learned in school while gaining hands-on exposure in a real working environment.

It also helped me improve my technical skills, discipline, teamwork, and readiness for future employment.
